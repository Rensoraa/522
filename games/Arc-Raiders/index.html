Arc Raiders

ARC Raiders is a multiplayer extraction adventure and action-shooter
set in a lethal, post-apocalyptic future Earth
ravaged by a mysterious mechanized threat known as the ARC.
In the game you take on the role of a Raider
- a scavenger optimizer and survivor - venturing from the underground refuge of Speranza
up onto the dangerous surface to scavenge valuable resources and gear
while battling deadly ARC machines and facing off against rival players.
